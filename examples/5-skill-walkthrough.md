# End-to-End 5-Skill Walkthrough

This short walkthrough shows how a learner uses all **5 skills** together during one week of learning **Negotiation** (a non-technical soft skill).

---

### Step 1 — Plan (`learning-roadmap-builder`)
> **User:** "I want to get better at salary & vendor negotiation in 4 weeks, 3 hours/week. I'm a mid-level PM and usually freeze when someone pushes back on price."

- The agent runs a 2-round diagnostic interview (probes on BATNA, anchoring, and interest-based bargaining), classifies the domain as **Physical / Soft Skills**, and generates a 4-week sprint roadmap with scripted roleplays and a 20-minute `[MVW]` drill per week.

---

### Step 2 — Understand (`socratic-tutor`)
> **User:** "Explain BATNA and ZOPA interactively — I keep mixing them up."

- Instead of a textbook definition, the agent anchors to PM work (*"BATNA is your fallback vendor if this deal falls through; ZOPA is the overlap between your max budget and their minimum acceptable price"*), draws a quick ASCII/Mermaid range, and asks one micro-check: *"If your BATNA gets stronger, does the ZOPA shrink or expand?"*

---

### Step 3 — Practice & Critique (`practice-critique-coach`)
> **User:** "Roleplay a 5-turn vendor renewal negotiation where they want to raise our SaaS contract by 25%, and grade me at the end."

- The agent stays in character as a firm enterprise account exec for 5 turns, introduces a mid-scene deadline curveball, then debriefs with a **1–3 Rubric Scorecard** (Anchoring: 2, Silence/Pacing: 1, Trade-off Framing: 3), quotes 2 exact missed lines with stronger rewrites, and prescribes a **10-minute drill** practicing 3 ways to pause and ask open questions after an aggressive anchor.

---

### Step 4 — Retrieve & Retain (`active-recall-quizzer`)
> **User:** "Quiz me on Week 1 concepts without showing answers first."

- The agent delivers 5 adaptive scenario questions one at a time, catches a confident mistake distinguishing *anchoring* vs *concession framing*, explains why the distractor failed, and schedules a **1-day spaced review** in `weak-areas-log.md`.

---

### Step 5 — Synthesize (`knowledge-synthesizer`)
> **User:** "Audit my Feynman explanation of interest-based bargaining, then build me a 1-page pre-meeting cheat sheet."

- The learner writes a 6-line plain-language explanation; the agent flags one vague phrase, helps sharpen it, and outputs a **60-second Pre-Negotiation Cheat Sheet** (5-step prep checklist + "If they say X → Respond with Y" trigger table) ready to glance at before real meetings.
