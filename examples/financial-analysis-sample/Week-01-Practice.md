## Lab 1 — Guided Practice: Trace One Transaction
> **Covers:** [Stage 1 — One transaction, three statements](Week-01-Reading-the-3-Statements.md#stage-1) | **Time:** 25 min

- **Scenario:** A SaaS company signs a \$120,000 annual contract on Jan 1, paid upfront in cash.
- **Task:** Record the exact impact on **Month 1** across:
  1. Income Statement (Revenue recognized)
  2. Cash Flow Statement (Cash received)
  3. Balance Sheet (Cash + Deferred Revenue)
- **Expected outcome:** Revenue = +\$10k, Cash = +\$120k, Deferred Revenue (Liability) = +\$110k. Notice how assets and liabilities stay balanced.
- **Next:** go back to [Stage 2](Week-01-Reading-the-3-Statements.md#stage-2) in the notes.

<a id="lab-2"></a>
## Lab 2 `[MVW]` — Drill A: Real-Company Cash vs Profit Teardown
> **Covers:** [Stage 2 — Why cash ≠ profit](Week-01-Reading-the-3-Statements.md#stage-2) | **Time:** 25 min

- **Scenario:** Open the latest annual report (10-K) of a company whose product you already use (e.g., Spotify, Netflix, or Costco) on SEC EDGAR or Yahoo Finance.
- **Task:** Extract 3 numbers for the last 2 years: **Net Income**, **Operating Cash Flow**, and **Free Cash Flow**. Write 3 bullet points explaining *why* Net Income and Operating Cash Flow differ.
- **Target check:** Identify at least 1 concrete driver (e.g., stock-based compensation, depreciation, or changes in deferred revenue/inventory).
- **Next:** go back to [Stage 3](Week-01-Reading-the-3-Statements.md#stage-3) in the notes.

<a id="lab-3"></a>
## Lab 3 — Drill B: Spot the "Profitable but Dying" Trap
> **Covers:** [Stage 3 — Working capital warning signs](Week-01-Reading-the-3-Statements.md#stage-3) | **Time:** 30 min

- **Scenario:** Company X grew Revenue +40% and Net Income +20% YoY, yet Operating Cash Flow turned negative while Accounts Receivable doubled.
- **Task:** Write a 5-line diagnostic hypothesis: what might be happening operationally, and what 2 follow-up numbers would you check next?
- **Next:** go back to [Stage 4](Week-01-Reading-the-3-Statements.md#stage-4) in the notes.

## Lab 4 — Transfer Challenge (Unguided)
> **Covers:** Stages 1–3 combined | **Time:** 45 min

### Scenario & Constraints
Pick **any** public company you've considered investing in. Using only its latest 3 statements, write a **150-word Health Snapshot** answering: *"Is this business actually generating cash, and is its balance sheet resilient in a downturn?"*

<details>
<summary>Hints (open only if stuck >15 min)</summary>

1. Compare Cash + Short-term Investments against Total Debt (Net Cash vs Net Debt).
2. Check whether Free Cash Flow covers interest expense and share buybacks/dividends.

</details>

### Self-Check Rubric
| Criterion | 1 (Needs work) | 2 (Solid) | 3 (Strong) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Evidence rigor** | Vague opinion, no numbers | Cites ≥2 numbers from statements | Cites ≥3 linked numbers across ≥2 statements |
| **Cash vs Profit distinction** | Treats Net Income as cash | Mentions cash flow separately | Explains *why* cash diverges from profit |
| **Risk awareness** | Only lists positives | Mentions 1 generic risk | Names 1 specific balance-sheet or cash risk |

### AI Critique Prompt
> Paste your 150-word Health Snapshot with this prompt into `practice-critique-coach`:
> *"Act as a strict equity research analyst. Grade my 150-word Health Snapshot against the 3 criteria above (Evidence rigor, Cash vs Profit distinction, Risk awareness). Return: (1) Score 1–3 per criterion, (2) Top 2 strengths, (3) Top 2 concrete fixes with rewritten lines, (4) One 10-minute follow-up drill."*
